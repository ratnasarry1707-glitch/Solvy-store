Questions, answered

Still curious? Our team replies within a few hours.

Do I need to invite a bot to my calls?

No. Lumen connects to your calendar and records through the meeting platform's own API, so nobody sees an extra participant.

Which languages does Lumen understand?

Thirty languages, including English, Bahasa Indonesia, Spanish, Japanese and German. It can also handle calls that switch between languages.

Who can see my meeting notes?

Only the people who attended, unless you choose to share. Admins can set workspace-wide rules on the Business plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from settings in two clicks and keep access until the end of your billing period.
