Good questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp, we reply fast.

Does my dog need vaccinations?

Yes. We ask for up-to-date rabies and core vaccines for the playground and grooming. Bring the vaccination book on your first visit.

How long does a full groom take?

Two to three hours depending on size and coat. We send a photo when they're done so you can finish your coffee.

Can nervous dogs come?

Of course. Book a quiet slot and we'll take it slow, with breaks and plenty of treats.
