Frequently asked questions

I can't code at all. Will this work for me?

Yes. Templates are edited like a document, and the prompt packs tell you exactly what to paste into AI. Our setup guide walks you through it in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

What's the difference between a template, a kit and a prompt pack?

A template is a finished website, a prompt pack teaches AI to build one with you, and a kit gives you both. The kit is the template plus the prompts and planning sheets to make it fully yours.

How do I receive my files?

Right after payment you get a download link on screen and by email, from Lynk.id or Gumroad. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

Can I use a template for a client?

Yes. Each purchase covers one website, yours or one client's. All-Access covers unlimited client sites.

What can I do with my purchase?

Use it for your own business or for one client website per purchase. You can't resell or share the files. The full terms are on our licence and refund page.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are limited: sales are final because files are delivered instantly. If a file is broken or not what the page described, message us within 7 days and we'll fix it, replace it, or refund you.

Can you build a website for me?

Yes. Besides templates and prompts, Ratna designs custom websites for brands. Send your business type and goals on WhatsApp and we'll reply with options and a quote. See the portfolio below.

Do I need a laptop?

No, a laptop isn't required: prompts and guides work on your phone. Editing a template is much easier on a laptop, though.

Is there a website template for a beauty clinic?

Yes, Lune is a website template made for beauty clinics, with before/after results, a skin check and online booking. If you also want the AI prompt system to rewrite it for your clinic, choose the Beauty Clinic Kit. See the Lune template page for the live demo.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features, so there is no fixed price. Send your business type, the pages you need and your budget on WhatsApp, and Ratna replies with options and a quote after a free consultation.

Can I publish my website for free with Netlify?

Yes. Netlify's free plan hosts your template at no cost: drag the whole template folder onto app.netlify.com/drop and it is live in about 10 seconds. A custom domain is optional and bought separately. Our step-by-step publishing guide shows every step.

What is the difference between a website template and a website kit?

A template is only the finished HTML website, while a kit is that same template plus an AI prompt system. A brand website kit adds a 13-page prompt book, an AI workflow guide, a client brief, a content planner and a launch checklist, so you can make the site fully yours.
