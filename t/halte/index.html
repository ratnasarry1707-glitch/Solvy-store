Meet the chef
"We wanted the kind of place you stop at on the way home and stay until closing."

Halte means stop. Chef Dewi Hartono opened it in 2022 with one oven, eighteen seats and a bar built from reclaimed teak. Before that she cooked for nine years in Jakarta and Melbourne. The oven's still the same. The seats aren't.
