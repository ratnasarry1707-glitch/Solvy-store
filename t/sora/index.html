How we think about a building

Four ideas behind every project, from a garden studio to a village library.

Climate first

Deep roofs, cross breezes and thermal mass before any machine.

Honest materials

Timber, stone and lime left visible, so they can patina instead of peel.

Local hands

We work with the same craftspeople for years and design around their skill.

Room to change

Plans that adapt as families grow, without knocking down walls.

Our material library

Every material we specify, we have used before and watched age for at least five years.
