I can't code at all. Will this work for me?

Yes. Templates are edited like a document, and the prompt packs tell you exactly what to paste into AI. Our setup guide walks you through it in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

What's the difference between a template, a kit and a prompt pack?

A template is a finished website. A prompt pack teaches AI to build one with you. A kit gives you both: the template plus the prompts and planning sheets to make it fully yours.

How do I receive my files?

Right after payment you get a download link on screen and by email, from Lynk.id or Gumroad. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

Can I use a template for a client?

Yes. Each purchase covers one website, yours or one client's. All-Access covers unlimited client sites.

What can I do with my purchase?

Use it for your own business or for one client website per purchase. You can't resell or share the files. The full terms are on our licence and refund page.

Can I get a refund?

Because files are delivered instantly, sales are final. If a file is broken or not what the page described, message us within 7 days and we'll fix it, replace it, or refund you.
