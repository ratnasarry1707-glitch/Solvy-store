    Questions

    Is the consultation really free?

    Yes. Your first 20-minute consultation and skin scan are free, with no obligation to book a treatment.

    Who performs the treatments?

    Injectables and lasers are performed only by our doctors. Facials are done by trained nurses under a doctor's supervision.

    Is there downtime?

    Most facials and pico toning have none. Fractional resurfacing can leave skin pink for two to three days. Your doctor will tell you before you book.

    Can I pay in instalments?

    Yes, packages can be paid with 0% instalments on most credit cards.
